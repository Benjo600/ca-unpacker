CA Unpacker is installed. Nothing is uploaded — client files stay on this PC.

Do this next:

1. Click Finish, then open CA Unpacker (Start menu or the desktop shortcut).

2. Type your firm name.

3. Click Choose folder and pick where cleaned Excels should go
   (Desktop or Documents is fine).

4. Click Open the desk.

5. Type a client name and click Add client.

6. Click that client. Type the month (for example Aug 2026) and click Add period.

7. Click the period. Click Add folder and choose the month’s files
   (bank PDFs, invoices, GSTR JSON, Tally/Zoho — they can go in together).

8. When the pack appears, click Open in Excel.

The app will keep pointing at the next button until you skip the guide.
You can open How to use again from the left rail at any time.
