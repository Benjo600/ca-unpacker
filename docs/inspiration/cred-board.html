What Cred actually does

Four transferable laws, separated from what only works because Cred sells rewards.

Black does the luxury

Cred's ground is near-black with depth, and its panels barely differ from it. Expensive surfaces come from layer tonality and hairlines, not from color variety. The lesson your incumbent graphite already half-knows, then goes further.

Numbers get display scale

Ordinary tables get ordinary type; the one number that matters tonight gets billboard size. A reconciled figure at display scale is a moment, not a metric, and that is what makes data feel like pride.

One accent, spent rarely

Cred holds a single precious accent in reserve and spends it only on the thing worth touching. Scarcity is what makes an accent read premium; spread across ten UI states, the same hue reads as decoration.

Every state feels physical

Selection presses in, queued work settles with weight, progress lands — motion confirms rather than decorates. This is the piece a filing-week tool can borrow shamelessly: reassurance is a feeling, delivered 400 times a day.
